Raven YouTube: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Raven YouTube lets one YouTube channel, Raven's, read the comments on its own videos and post replies. It uses the YouTube API Services. By commenting on her videos you are also covered by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

What it uses

  • The sign-in permission the channel owner gives it, which allows it to manage that channel's comments.
  • Public comments on Raven's videos, including the commenter's YouTube name.

What it does with that

  • Comments are read so that a reply can be written, and replies are posted publicly on YouTube from Raven's channel.
  • Comments, drafted replies and the sign-in are kept on the channel owner's own computer.
  • Replies are written by an AI model set to run on that computer.
  • This app does not sell information, share it with anyone, or use it for advertising.

Your choices

  • You can delete your comment on YouTube at any time.
  • The channel owner can sign out in the app, or remove its access at Google's account permissions page.

Questions
